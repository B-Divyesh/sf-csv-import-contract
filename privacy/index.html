⌗ CSV / import contract

Policy sheet · effective 27 August 2026

Privacy

CSV Import Contract is designed for sensitive migration work. Source files, mappings, rules, and generated artifacts are processed in your browser and are not uploaded to us.

Data stored on your device

The current project is stored in your browser’s IndexedDB so work survives a refresh or closed tab. A purchased license token and its most recent verification result are stored in localStorage. You can remove project data with “Replace source” and can clear all site data through your browser settings.

Network requests

The free workbench makes no data or analytics requests. The app checks for static updates through its service worker. If you paste or receive a paid license, the license token is sent to the Sociobot billing API solely to verify access. Your imported file and contract are never included.

Payments

Sociobot/Dodo is the merchant of record. Checkout happens on their hosted page and is governed by the information presented there. We never receive card details.

Your control

You can export the contract, cleaned CSV, issue evidence, and report at any time. Browser storage stays on the device and is not synchronized. Uninstalling the PWA or clearing site data may remove it, so retain exported artifacts in your approved records system.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the support contact on sociobot.in.
